DM keywords

Comment-to-DM · one per IG piece
VALUE

A one-on-one value review within 24 hours. Never post a number in public.

Ask: "Thinking about selling in the next 12 months, or just want to know where you stand?"

NET

A side-by-side net sheet: price cut vs. seller credit or buydown, using a lender quote, within 24 hours.

Ask: "Is the house listed now, or are you getting ready to list?"

PAYMENT

A real monthly payment estimate with a lender-quoted rate, within 24 hours.

Ask: "Are you pre-approved yet, or still early?"

BRIDGE

Sell here, buy in the DR. The conversation covers the Pocono sale side: value, timing, net proceeds.

Non-negotiables

The short list

Voice

Bronx cadence, direct, one take. Hook in the first 2 seconds, stated as a statement. Banned: "game changer," "dream home," "navigate the market," "in today's ever-changing market," rhetorical questions, generic CTAs.

Accuracy

Never invent stats, rates, law status, or program rules. Every time-sensitive claim goes in the packet's verify table.

One topic, one home

Market goes in the Ledger and everything else adapts it. PIR pulls from research. DR has no written home.

Platform-native

No shared captions. Every video goes to YouTube with a full AEO description. Nextdoor is neighborly: no pitch, no keyword, no hashtags.

GBP

No hashtags, no client names or addresses. Real photos only. License block on every post.

Spanish + privacy

Spanish is written fresh in Dominican cadence, never translated. Clients are buyer/seller + town only.
